# Code of Conduct

We expect participants to treat others with respect, welcome good-faith feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publication of someone else's private information without permission.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and discussions that violate these standards. Repeated or severe violations may lead to a temporary or permanent participation ban. Do not publish sensitive reports or personal information in public discussions.

Report a concern through the repository's available moderation channel. No private reporting address is currently designated.

This policy adapts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its official text provides further enforcement guidance.
